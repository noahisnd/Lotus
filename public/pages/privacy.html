Privacy

Nothing in Stick sends anything to us: no account, no check to see whether you paid, no record of how you use it.

What stays on your machine

Your modes, your blocked lists and your session history.

What it downloads

Two lists from GitHub, neither carrying anything about you: the sites for a category when you switch one on, and a weekly refresh of the list of sites people use to get around blockers. Any network monitor will show you exactly that.
